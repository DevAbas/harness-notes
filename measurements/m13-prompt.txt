There are two saved-view mechanisms in this app and there should be one.

## What is there now

The ticket queue saves a filter combination under a name. The customer list does
the same thing, separately: its own storage, its own dialog, its own active and
modified logic, written by copying the ticket one and changing the strings.

Two copies of one idea, and the second already carries the first one's rough
edges. A third screen would make three.

## What is needed

One mechanism. A saved view is a named set of filters that belongs to a screen,
and the screen asks for the views that are its own.

Adding saved views to a third screen should mean describing that screen's
filters, not writing a third sidebar and a third dialog.

## What is already decided

The two screens keep the behaviour they have today. A person who saved views on
either one still has them, still sees them in the same place, and still sees the
same active and modified states. This is a change to how it is built, not to
what it does.

The filters on the two screens are genuinely different shapes and stay that way.
Whatever is shared cannot flatten them into one type that is wrong for both.

localStorage stays. What is stored may change shape if it has to, but what is
already stored cannot be lost.

## What is not decided

Where the shared mechanism lives and what it is called. Whether the sidebar, the
dialog and the naming flow are one thing or several. How a screen describes its
filters to it. What happens to the two existing storage keys. Whether the
comparison that produces the modified state can be shared or has to stay
per-screen.

Those are design decisions and I want yours. AGENTS.md and the design system
carry what this app has already decided; anything they do not answer is open,
and if you find yourself wanting a primitive that does not exist, say so rather
than assembling one in the feature.

## Constraints

- Read AGENTS.md before you start.
- Strict TypeScript, no `any`.
- Tests for the shared mechanism, and both screens' existing tests must still
  pass unchanged where they describe behaviour.
- All existing tests must pass and the count must not drop.
- Run typecheck, lint, tests and lint:boundaries at the end and report what
  passes.
- Do not commit.
